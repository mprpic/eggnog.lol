          How to make it

          Phase 1: day 0

          1. Separate the eggs. Only the yolks go in.
          2. Combine the rum, cognac and bourbon in something with a pour spout.
          3. Blend the yolks, the first half of the milk, two thirds of the nutmeg, the sugar and the salt at low speed until smooth, using a regular or immersion blender. Keep the speed down so it does not foam.
          4. With the blender running, pour in the spirits slowly. Alcohol added too fast is hard on the yolks.
          5. Transfer to Glass seals tightly and is easy to shake, which is worth doing every now and then. A few small jars fit a crowded fridge better than one big one., seal tightly and refrigerate for 30 days.

          Phase 2: day 30

          1. Pour the jars into one large container; the volume is about to nearly double. With the blender running at medium speed, slowly add the heavy cream, the half-and-half, the rest of the milk and the remaining nutmeg, and blend until smooth.
          2. Divide between jars again, seal and refrigerate for another 60 days.
          3. Serve cold, with fresh nutmeg grated over the top.

          2. Whisk the yolks with the sugar and nutmeg until pale and thick enough to trail off the whisk in ribbons.
          3. In a second bowl or pitcher, stir together the half-and-half, milk, cream, rum, cognac, bourbon and salt.
          4. Pour the liquid into the yolks a little at a time, whisking as you go.
          5. Transfer to Glass seals tightly and is easy to shake, which is worth doing every now and then. A few small jars fit a crowded fridge better than one big one., seal tightly and refrigerate.
          6. Leave it in the fridge for at least 2 weeks, and longer if you can wait.
          7. Serve cold, with fresh nutmeg grated over the top.

          A note on raw eggs. This recipe uses raw egg yolks. Studies of alcohol and Salmonella in egg drinks suggest that this much alcohol and weeks in the fridge kill it off. Consume at your own risk. To be extra careful, heat-treat the eggs first.
